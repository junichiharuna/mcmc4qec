# Initialization

These helpers produce candidate states from logical sectors or cosets. A candidate must still lie in your noise support and satisfy your actual decoder's failure condition. See the [initialization tutorial](../tutorial_en.md#initialization-cache-and-diagnostics) for use with conditional chains and the limits of weight-based diagnostics.
